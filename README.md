Bienvenue chez Pratt & Whitney Canada ! 😊 Intégrer une nouvelle entreprise peut être intimidant, c’est pourquoi le Comité des stagiaires est là pour vous accompagner et rendre votre expérience mémorable.
 
Qu’est-ce que le Comité des Stagiaires ? 

Nous sommes un groupe dédié de stagiaires dont l’objectif est d’améliorer votre expérience de stage chez P&WC. Nous organisons une variété d’activités, notamment des événements de réseautage, des présentations avec des gestionnaires, des rencontres sociales, des visites de plant, et plus encore. 

Rejoindre le Comité, c’est l’occasion idéale de développer votre réseau et de vivre une expérience enrichissante.

Hello to all Summer 2026 interns,

Welcome to Pratt & Whitney Canada! 😊 Starting at a new company can be intimidating, which is why the Intern Committee is here to support you and make your experience unforgettable.

What is the Intern Committee?

We are a dedicated group of interns whose goal is to enhance your internship experience at P&WC. We organize a variety of activities, including networking events, presentations with managers, social gatherings, plant tours, and more.

Joining the Committee is the perfect opportunity to expand your network and have a rewarding experience.

HOW TO JOIN THE INTERN COMMITTEE:
To become a Plant Representative, send us your application:

Who are we looking for?
•	5-7 Representatives for Plant 1 (Longueuil).
•	1-2 Representatives for Plant 22 (Mississauga).
•	1 Representative each from all other P&WC Plants (5, 15, 23, 32, 41).

Roles and Responsibilities

•	Manage and plan your plant's activities, follow up with members, organize events and ensure visibility.  
•	Ensure that ideas submitted by members of the intern committee are carried out.  
•	Participate in weekly meetings with other representatives.  
•	Make decisions that will have a direct impact on the committee's future. 
•	Maintain and develop partnerships with various stakeholders within P&WC. 
•	Manage communications and platforms such as Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Intranet Page, etc.  
•	Manage the finances of the Intern Committee.  
•	Ensure the continuity of the committee after you leave!!!!  
o	Recruit representatives for the next Intern Committee executive team. 
o	Document processes to facilitate the handover for the next session's succession. 

Why apply?
•	Enhance your résumé and build a privileged network.
•	Participate in planning exciting events.
•	Contribute to improving the intern experience.

Important Note:
•	The Intern Committee Representative role is voluntary and sits outside your normal work responsibilities. Before applying, ensure you have spoken with your manager and received their approval to participate.
